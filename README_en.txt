============================================================
 PetApp2 Portable - English Guide
============================================================

PetApp2 Portable is an AI-powered desktop pet application.
You can register images and videos of your own pets (dogs, cats,
rabbits, etc.) and let them move, react, and express emotions
on your desktop.

This portable edition includes an embedded Python environment.
No installation is required. Simply extract the ZIP and run
"run.bat" to start the application.

------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Features
------------------------------------------------------------

• 15 pet states (n1–n3, p1–p12)
  - Neutral states: n1 (normal), n2 (rest), n3 (sleep)
  - Positive states: p1–p12 (play, happy, greeting, eating,
    walking, relaxing, etc.)

• Face recognition
  - The pet reacts when the user smiles.

• Voice recognition
  - The pet responds to simple voice interactions.

• AI image/video generation support
  - Includes guides for Gemini, Copilot, and Pika.
  - Helps users generate missing images or videos externally.

• **Automatic prompt generation (English & Japanese)**
  - The app automatically generates optimized prompts for all
    15 states (n1–n3, p1–p12).
  - Prompts are based on your pet’s profile (name, species,
    color, features) and the selected state.
  - Prompts can be copied directly into external AI tools to
    generate images or videos.

• Portable Python included
  - Works without installing Python on your system.

------------------------------------------------------------
 2. How to Use
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Run "run.bat".
3. Register your pet’s profile (name, species, color, etc.).
4. Assign images/videos to each state (n1–n3, p1–p12).
5. Use the AI Generation Guide to create missing materials.
6. Open the Play Window to see your pet move and react.

------------------------------------------------------------
 3. System Requirements
------------------------------------------------------------

• OS: Windows 10 / Windows 11  
• GPU: Not required (runs on CPU)  
• Python: Embedded Python included  
• Installation: Not required (ZIP only)

------------------------------------------------------------
 4. Important Note About Pet States
------------------------------------------------------------

This application is designed around the themes of
“connection, comfort, and love” between people and their pets.

Negative or harmful states such as anger, fear, sadness,
aggression, bullying, or abuse are intentionally excluded
from the app’s design and behavior.

All 15 states are neutral or positive to ensure a gentle,
healing, and emotionally safe experience.
